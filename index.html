How to play (tap to expand) Every board has its own rule, shown above the board — each one a different kind of thinking puzzle. Word Groups: two words from the same group. Word Pairs: a word and its partner (cat → kitten, hot → cold). Secret Number Rule: work out the rule from the examples, then match a number with its answer. Shape Rule: two pictures that share the rule (same shape, same shading, same number of sides…). Mirror Twins: a picture and its mirror image. Turn Twins: the same picture turned round (flipped doesn't count!). Tap two tiles that match. They must be in the same row, column, or a straight diagonal, with no other tile blocking the path between them — cleared spaces AND hearts don't block, so a match can connect straight through a heart. Do that and the heart pops for +10 (watch it float to the top of the board) plus a shuffle token, for each heart crossed. The board wraps around on every edge (left-right and top-bottom, including diagonals). Clear every number in a row to pop its hearts for bonus points. Shuffling the remaining tiles costs one shuffle token — no tokens, no shuffle — and tokens reset whenever a new board is generated. Pressing New Board means answering 5 puzzle questions correctly first (a mistake shows why, then asks again), then spinning a wheel to pick the next kind of board — no skipping either step. Out of shuffle tokens? The same 5-question quiz earns you one shuffle. After a match, everything above the gap — numbers and hearts alike — falls down to fill it. Every so often a match triggers a quick bonus question about the same kind of thinking — get it right for extra points.
